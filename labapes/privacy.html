Lab Apes Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 3, 2026. Lab Apes is a virtual reality game for Meta Quest made by GunnerGamesStudios ("we").

What we collect and why

  • An anonymous player ID. When you play online, Unity Gaming Services signs you in anonymously and gives your copy of the game a random ID. It is used to put you in rooms with other players and to keep your mute and block lists working.
  • Your in-game name and ape. Other players in your room see your ape's name, look, position and movements.
  • Voice chat. If your microphone is on, your voice is sent live, through Unity's Vivox voice service, to players near your ape in the same room. We do not record or store voice chat. You can mute your microphone, and mute or block any player, from the ONLINE board in the game.
  • Your Meta account. The game uses Meta's platform to check that you own the game. It may also read your Meta username to recognize the game's staff. If you use the Report button in the Quest menu, Meta handles that report under Meta's own policies, which can include a recording of recent voice chat.
  • Your progress. Vials, levels, outfits, settings, and photos or videos you take with the Lab Cam are saved only on your headset. We don't receive them.

What we don't do

No ads. We don't sell or rent your information. We don't use analytics or tracking tools.

Services we use

  • Unity Gaming Services (sign-in, multiplayer rooms, Vivox voice chat): Unity's privacy policy
  • Meta Platform (ownership check, username, reporting): Meta's privacy policy

Children

Lab Apes is rated for teens and adults (13+) on the Meta Horizon Store. It is not directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect personal information from them. If you believe a child under 13 has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

Keeping and deleting your data

The anonymous ID and room information are only used while you play online. To delete your progress, uninstall the game, which erases the saves on your headset. To ask us anything, or to ask us to delete information, email us.
