About GunnerVerse

The giant open world built by Gamer Gunner. Battle through a 30-chapter Story, take on holiday bosses, win tokens at the arcade, ride dirt bikes, explore downtown and play with friends using a code.

▶ Play now

Free. Plays in your browser on a computer or an iPad with a keyboard.

What's in the game

📖

30-chapter Story

Wake up in a strange town and battle through 30 chapters, from the desert temple and the volcano to the sky and the final fight against THE GALAXY.

⚔️

Boss fights

Every chapter has its own boss that drops a signature weapon and armor. Holiday bosses, Gunner's Mom and Mrs. McClendon show up in Sandbox, and the Boss Arena lets you pick two fighters.

🕹️

The arcade

11 games, including retro cabinets, pinball, skee-ball, a dance machine, a racing sim and VR. Win tokens to adopt pets at the pound and grab prizes.

🏍️

Cars + dirt bikes

Pick a car in the garage, upgrade it at the GG Auto Shop, buy a dirt bike, hit the jumps at the MX Park and race your friends at the Speedway.

🏙️

A whole town

Themed houses with backyard games, downtown towers with rooftop runs, Mom's house with Gunner's family, the Skattebo house with a secret skin, caves and mining.

🤝

Play together

Press U to make a code. Friends type it in and join your world in Sandbox, or team up in Story Mode to beat chapters and bosses together.

For parents

  • A username, not a real name. Players pick a username (it can't be changed) and a display name. There's no email, password, birthday or other personal info. The account is saved in this browser.
  • Friends, with no private messages. Players can send a friend request to a @username, and the other player can accept or decline. Friends see each other's display name, whether they're online or playing GunnerVerse, and can join each other's Sandbox game. There is no 1-on-1 messaging. Anyone can Unfriend or Block a player at any time, and a blocked player can't send requests again.
  • Playing together is optional. Share a 4-digit code, or join a friend from the Friends list. Open games are listed in the game unless the host locks the room. Up to 8 players, the host can kick, ban and lock, and you can leave any time.
  • Chat is only inside a game room. Players in the same room can send short text messages (80 characters). Links and phone numbers are blocked, bad words are starred out, and the room's host can mute anyone. Playing solo has no chat at all.
  • Player-made games. Players can build games in GunnerVerse Studio and publish them with a title, description and picture. They go live right away. Links and phone numbers are removed and bad words are starred out in the title, description and words inside the game, but pictures are not checked before they appear. Anyone signed in can Report a game, and Gamer Gunner's team can remove games and ban accounts.
  • No ads and no purchases. Coins and tokens are earned by playing, never bought, and daily limits keep it fair. (The Shop link goes to the separate merch store.)
  • What goes online. Game progress is saved in this browser only. In a shared game, your character's position, look, name and chat messages go to the other players. A signed-in player's game checks in every 30 seconds so friends can see they're playing. Likes and play counts are counted on our server.
  • Kid-friendly action. Blocky bad guys crumble into dust. No blood.

Tip for iPad: tap Play now, then in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen. It opens full-screen like an app, and Safari keeps the saves (Safari can clear saved games for sites you haven't visited in a week).
