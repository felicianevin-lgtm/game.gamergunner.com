The Game

A giant open world built by Gamer Gunner. Battle through a 30-chapter Story, take on holiday bosses, win tokens at the arcade, ride dirt bikes, explore downtown and play with friends using a code.

▶ Play now

Free. Plays in your browser on a computer or an iPad with a keyboard.

What's in the game

📖

30-chapter Story

Wake up in a strange town and battle through 30 chapters, from the desert temple and the volcano to the sky and the final fight against THE GALAXY.

⚔️

Boss fights

Every chapter has its own boss that drops a signature weapon and armor. Holiday bosses, Gunner's Mom and Mrs. McClendon show up in Sandbox, and the Boss Arena lets you pick two fighters.

🕹️

The arcade

11 games, including retro cabinets, pinball, skee-ball, a dance machine, a racing sim and VR. Win tokens to adopt pets at the pound and grab prizes.

🏍️

Cars + dirt bikes

Pick a car in the garage, upgrade it at the GG Auto Shop, buy a dirt bike, hit the jumps at the MX Park and race your friends at the Speedway.

🏙️

A whole town

Themed houses with backyard games, downtown towers with rooftop runs, Mom's house with Gunner's family, the Skattebo house with a secret skin, caves and mining.

🤝

Play together

Press U to make a code. Friends type it in and join your world in Sandbox, or team up in Story Mode to beat chapters and bosses together.

For parents

  • Only people with your code. Playing together is optional and needs a code you share yourself. Everyone gets a random name like "Green Fox", never their real name. Up to 8 players, and you can leave any time.
  • Chat is only inside a code room. Players in the same room can send short text messages (80 characters). Links are blocked, bad words are starred out, and the room's host can mute anyone. Playing solo has no chat at all.
  • No ads, no purchases and no sign-up in the game. Coins and tokens are earned by playing, never bought, and daily limits keep it fair. (The Shop link in the menu goes to the separate merch store.)
  • Saves stay on your device. Progress is stored in this browser only. In a shared game, only your character's position, look, the random name and chat messages go to the other players. The game also sends an anonymous "someone is playing" check-in so we can count players online. It has no name or personal info.
  • Kid-friendly action. Blocky bad guys crumble into dust. No blood.

Tip for iPad: tap Play now, then in Safari tap Share → Add to Home Screen. It opens full-screen like an app, and Safari keeps the saves (Safari can clear saved games for sites you haven't visited in a week).
