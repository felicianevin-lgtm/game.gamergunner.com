The Game

A giant open world built by Gamer Gunner. Explore the town, drive cars, dig through caves, take on the Story, and play with friends using a code.

▶ Play now

Free. Plays in your browser on a computer or an iPad with a keyboard.

What's in the game

📖

Story mode

Wake up in a strange town, help the stranger, earn your sword, and find out where your crew went.

🏜️

Boss fights

Cross the desert to the old temple and face the Sand Guardian. Watch for the slam!

🚗

Cars + garage

Jump in, drive the open world, and pick your ride in the garage.

⛏️

Caves + mining

Three cave networks full of Stone, Water and rare Dragon Crystals. Craft armor from what you dig up.

🤝

Play together

In Sandbox, press U to make a code like GG-48213. Friends type it in and they're in your world.

🧍

Your avatar

Pick your skin, shirt, pants, hair and hat. Your look saves automatically.

For parents

  • No chat, ever. Kids can't send messages or type names to each other.
  • Only friends with your code. Playing together is optional and only works with a 5-digit code you share yourself. Everyone gets a random name like "Green Fox". Up to 8 players.
  • No ads, no purchases, no sign-up. Just press play.
  • Saves stay on your device. Progress is stored in this browser only. In a shared game, only your character's position and colors go to the other players.
  • Kid-friendly action. Blocky bad guys crumble into dust. No blood.

Tip for iPad: open the game in Safari, tap Share → Add to Home Screen, and it opens full-screen like an app.
